# Billing Policy

## Consultation Fees
General Medicine consultation costs Rs. 500.
Specialist consultation costs Rs. 800.

## Diagnostic Charges
A basic blood test costs Rs. 300.
An X-ray costs Rs. 600.

## Payment Methods
Payments can be made by cash, card or supported digital payment methods.

## Refunds
Billing disputes and refund requests must be submitted to the billing department with the receipt.
