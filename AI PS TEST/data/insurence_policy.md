# Health Insurance Policy

## Insurance Desk
The insurance desk operates from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## Required Documents
Patients should bring their health insurance card, government-issued identification and relevant medical documents.

## Cashless Treatment
Cashless treatment depends on the insurance provider, policy coverage and hospital authorization.

## Claim Assistance
Patients can contact the insurance desk for claim forms, coverage verification and claim-status assistance.

## Important Notice
Coverage and approval are determined by the insurance provider. The hospital helpdesk cannot guarantee claim approval.
