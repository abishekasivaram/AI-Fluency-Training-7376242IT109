# Hospital Services

## Departments
The hospital has General Medicine, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics and Dermatology departments.

## Working Hours
Outpatient departments operate from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

## Diagnostic Services
Laboratory tests and X-ray services are available from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## Helpdesk
Patients can contact the hospital helpdesk at the main reception for department information and general assistance.
