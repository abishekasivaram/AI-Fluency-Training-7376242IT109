# Appointment Policy

## Booking
Patients can book appointments at the reception desk or through the hospital's online appointment portal.

## Cancellation
Appointments should be cancelled at least 4 hours before the scheduled time.

## Late Arrival
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule, depending on doctor availability.

## Walk-in Patients
Walk-in consultations are subject to doctor availability and waiting time.
